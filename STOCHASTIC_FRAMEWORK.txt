## MOSEK'S BASED ON KUHN 


init: creates instance(N_simulations,rRange)


analytic_out_perf(x_dec,testSampleSet): calculate Joos of decision on sampleSet


solve(radius): solves model with radius, get x_dec. Runs analytic_out_perf(). returns (x_dec, Joos, rel:=(Joos<=Jis)) point


iter_radius_on_solve(rRange): for r in rRange yield solve(r)


simulate(trainData): sets the model's trainData and iterates over iter_radius(), returns x_dec, Joos, rel vectors


iter_data_on_simulate(trainDataSets): for dataset in trainDataSet yield simulate(dataset)

run_sim(trainDataSets): call iter_data(trainDataSets), get x_dec_mean, Joos_avg, Joos_q20, Joos_q80, rel_mean
